Maintenance knowledge shouldn’t be hard to find.

Technicians spend significant time and effort searching through equipment manuals, specifications, datasheets and maintenance procedures while repairing industrial equipment – time that can extend maintenance sessions and delay equipment return to service. Under pressure to restore equipment quickly, teams may also depend heavily on experienced technicians or OEM representatives, or overlook recommended procedures altogether. No more manual searching, no more guesswork.

The information already exists. It is simply difficult to access.

Onesi is an AI maintenance companion built for industrial teams. Instead of manually searching through technical documentation, technicians ask natural-language questions and get relevant equipment information back through an AI-powered interface – pulled from manuals, datasheets and maintenance procedures.
